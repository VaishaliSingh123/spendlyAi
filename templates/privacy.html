Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly we collect the information you give us: your name, email address and password (stored in protected form), and the expense records you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to run your account, show your spending summaries and filters, and keep the service working and secure. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Spendly's database. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss or misuse, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure. We keep your data for as long as your account is active; you can ask us to delete your account and its records.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic request information such as your IP address under Google's own privacy policy. We do not otherwise share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Your Rights

You can review, correct or delete the information in your account at any time. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

6. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or how we handle your data can be sent to the Spendly team through the contact details published on this site.
